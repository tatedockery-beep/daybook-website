How it works

Three steps to your first paid job.

1

Sign up, pick your trade

Your starter kit loads: templates, catalog, inventory. No setup marathon.

2

Send your first estimate

Add a client, build the estimate from a template, email it. They approve with one click.

3

Job → invoice → paid

The approved estimate becomes a scheduled job. The finished job becomes an invoice. The invoice takes card payments.

Your data is yours

Every business on Daybook is fully separated. Nobody at another company can ever see your clients, prices, or numbers.

Two-factor authentication

Protect your account with an authenticator app. Simple to turn on in settings.

Leave anytime, with everything

Export all of your data, or delete your account and everything in it, straight from settings. No phone call required.
